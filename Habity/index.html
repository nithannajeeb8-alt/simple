BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Privacy, plainly.

Your profile and daily records stay in this browser by default. External lookups work differently; here is the distinction.

Your data and your choices
  • Stored in this browser: your profile, habits, moods, memories and preferences stay on this device. Little Days has no account or automatic sync.
  • Not encrypted: browser storage is not a secure vault. Anyone who can access this browser may be able to see your records, so be careful on shared devices.
  • Online lookups share queries: book, food, exercise and image searches send the terms you enter to that provider. Prayer-time lookup sends the city you choose. Each provider has its own privacy policy.
  • Backups are not protected: exported JSON files are unencrypted. Store them privately. Deleting browser data does not delete exported files or data already held by external services.
  • You stay in control: edit your profile, export or restore a backup, and delete local records in Settings. Daily tracking can work offline after loading; online lookups need internet access.
