Field notes

Three laws of systematic alignment.

Material

Dust isn't noise.

Grain and drift are what make a system feel inhabited instead of generated. We shape particles; we give them physics and purpose.

Timing

Fluidity is truth.

Good motion reveals itself the way a magnetic field does — gradually, responding to unseen forces, on a perfectly aligned schedule.

Restraint

Calculated subtraction.

Everything around everywhere should have a purpose. If a pixel or a motion doesn't earn its place systematically, it is removed entirely.

We spend most of our time looking at things too slowly for anyone else to notice — the way quantum dust catches light, the way particles avoid collision. Then we build software that remembers to do the same.
