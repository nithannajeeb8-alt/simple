Field notes

Three things we keep relearning.

Material

Noise isn't an error.

Grain and drift are what make a system feel inhabited instead of generated. We shape noise; we don't sand it off.

Timing

Nothing arrives all at once.

Good motion reveals itself the way weather does — gradually, unevenly, on its own schedule, not a fixed easing curve.

Restraint

Most of the work is subtraction.

The version that ships is usually the third one we removed things from. If a motion doesn't earn its place, it doesn't survive.

We spend most of our time looking at things too slowly for anyone else to notice — the way light travels across a wall, the way ink gives up its edge in water. Then we build software that remembers to do the same.

— the studio, most days
