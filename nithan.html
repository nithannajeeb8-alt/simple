Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I’m Nithan Najeeb, a commerce student with a growing practice in digital design and creative technology. I enjoy moving between structure and experimentation: understanding the problem, shaping the experience, and refining the details until everything feels connected.

I’m especially drawn to clean interfaces, strong typography, thoughtful motion and the new creative possibilities opened up by AI.
