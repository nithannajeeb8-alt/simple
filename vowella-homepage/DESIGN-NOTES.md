# Vowella design notes

## Direction

The page uses an art-directed, editorial direction rather than a stock wedding-builder layout: an olive hedge and cinematic couple reveal; paper-toned storytelling; a wine-red belief section; a marine-blue invitation cover flow; then a warm creator story and dark marine footer. Typography pairs a high-contrast display serif and an italic serif with a quiet sans-serif for labels and body copy. Gold is an accent, not a page-wide effect.

The layout alternates background colors and composition deliberately. Sections have different pacing and scale, images are treated as part of the story, and large whitespace is used to create hierarchy rather than filling every region with feature cards.

## Research and inspiration

These references informed the design principles. The site uses them as direction, not as copied layouts or assets.

- **Nithan.in** — source reference for cinematic image entry, intentional section pacing, expressive motion and an editorial portfolio structure: https://nithan.in/
- **Existing S1 invitation** — source of the real available invitation preview (Aarav & Meera): https://nithan.in/S1
- **Riley & Grey** — a design-led wedding website service; informed the balance between an elevated visual identity and clear, practical guest-facing information: https://www.rileygrey.com/
- **Zola's wedding website guide** — emphasizes design customization, mobile usability, and keeping the important information accessible: https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/best-wedding-websites
- **Greenvelope's 2026 comparison** — helped frame the invitation as a distinct experience connected to the couple's event details, rather than merely a decorative landing page: https://www.greenvelope.com/resources/best-wedding-websites
- **Nielsen Norman Group visual design research** — reinforces visual hierarchy, whitespace, and restraint so attractive interfaces don't become visually noisy: https://media.nngroup.com/media/reports/free/Designing_for_Young_Adults_3rd_Edition.pdf
- **Google Search Central** — stresses accuracy, usefulness and context for page metadata and automatically generated web content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content
- **MDN: `prefers-reduced-motion`** — informs the reduced-motion fallback for the entrance and scroll animations: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40media

## Specific choices made to avoid generic AI-site patterns

- No repeated three-column feature-card grid as the main structure.
- No invented customer testimonials, fake template counts, fabricated contact details, or placeholder statistics.
- Different sections use different composition, color, typography, and rhythm instead of copying the same rounded rectangle pattern everywhere.
- The cover flow distinguishes the one live invitation from designs that have not been made yet.
- Copy describes the project's intention without claiming unbuilt editor, payment, RSVP, or account features already work.
- Motion is used for the opening reveal and small content entrances; reduced-motion settings are respected.
- Images supplied for the project are used as the visual material throughout the page rather than replaced with generic external stock imagery.
