How it works

Three unhurried steps.

  1. i

    Discover

    Walk through the collection and open any design as a live invitation, on your own phone.

  2. ii

    Personalise

    Your names, dates and venue take the place of the sample details. Personalisation is still being prepared, so for now every design is a preview.

  3. iii

    Share

    An invitation is just a link. Send it to family and friends and it opens in any phone browser.

Our idea

A wedding card is held once. A wedding website is opened again and again.

Vowella began with a simple wish: to make invitations that feel personal, that couples are proud to send, and that guests return to for the date, the venue and the little details.

Each design is made by hand, with the same attention you would give a printed card.
