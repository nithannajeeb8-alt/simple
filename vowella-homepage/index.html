04A little about us

Made with care, not a factory line

A small idea
with a lot of heart.

Vowella is an independent project by Nithan Najeeb, created at the meeting point of design, storytelling and the web.

The aim is simple: make it easier for couples to share their day through a website that feels personal, polished and welcoming. Every flourish should have a reason. Every page should leave enough space for the people at its centre.

It’s being built one thoughtful detail at a time, with the hope that a beautiful invitation can feel special without feeling out of reach.
