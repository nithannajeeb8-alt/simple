Project 01 · Personal space

A place to keep your thoughts.

MyDiary is a personal journaling space designed for recording notes, keeping memories and reflecting over time. Its focus is a calm, private-feeling writing experience rather than a noisy social feed.

The project is developed and in the final refinement stage, with remaining bugs and small fixes to address before it is considered fully polished.

Project 02 · Practical utility

From images to a shareable document.

PDF Maker is a focused tool for turning images into a PDF without making the process feel complicated. The aim is a simple workflow that helps people prepare a document from image files quickly.

The core project is built. The remaining work is bug fixing and a small set of refinements to make the experience more reliable and consistent.

Project 03 · Personal finance

Make everyday money easier to understand.

PocketWise is a personal finance project centered on expense tracking, budgeting and building a clearer picture of spending habits. It is intended to help turn everyday money activity into information that is easier to review and act on.

The product is developed, with bugs and a few final fixes still remaining. This page describes its intended purpose without claiming specific features that have not been confirmed.
